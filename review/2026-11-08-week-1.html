Mixed quiz: week 1

Chemistry1. Which of these is a homogeneous mixture?

Air has the same composition throughout and one phase. The others have visible regions with different composition (heterogeneous).

Maths2. An arithmetic sequence has u1 = 5 and d = −3. Find u10.

u10 = 5 + 9 × (−3) = −22. −25 uses 10 steps instead of 9; 32 adds instead of subtracting.

Chemistry3. Which technique separates the dyes in a sample of ink?

The dyes are attracted differently to the paper (stationary phase) and the solvent (mobile phase), so they move different distances.

Maths4. Find the sum of the first 10 terms of 2 + 5 + 8 + …

S10 = 10/2 (2 × 2 + 9 × 3) = 5 × 31 = 155. 140 and 170 use 8 or 10 differences instead of 9; 310 forgets to halve.

Chemistry5. −73 °C in kelvin is

T(K) = T(°C) + 273 = −73 + 273 = 200 K. Kelvin temperatures are never negative.

Maths6. The 6th term of the geometric sequence 81, 27, 9, … is

r = 13, u6 = 81 × (13)5 = 81243 = 13. 19 uses the power 6 instead of 5.

Chemistry7. How many protons, neutrons and electrons are in 27Al3+? (Z = 13)

13 protons; 27 − 13 = 14 neutrons; 13 − 3 = 10 electrons. A 3+ ion has lost electrons, not gained them.

Maths8. The sum to infinity of 81 + 27 + 9 + … is

r = 13, so S∞ = 811 − 1/3 = 81 × 32 = 121.5. 243 divides by r instead of 1 − r.

Chemistry9. An element is 90% mass 20 and 10% mass 22. Its relative atomic mass is

(20 × 90 + 22 × 10) ÷ 100 = 2020 ÷ 100 = 20.2. It must lie close to the more abundant isotope (20). 21.0 is a plain average that ignores abundance.

Maths10. Σ from r = 1 to 5 of 2r equals

The terms are 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 (start at r = 1, so the first term is 2), total 62. 31 is 1 + 2 + 4 + 8 + 16, starting from 20.

Chemistry11. Which transition in a hydrogen atom emits visible light?

Falls to n = 2 give the visible lines. Falls to n = 1 give ultraviolet, falls to n = 3 give infrared, and n = 1 to n = 2 is absorption, not emission.

Maths12. The geometric series with first term 1 and ratio x3 has a sum to infinity when

The condition is |r| < 1, so |x/3| < 1, which is |x| < 3, that is −3 < x < 3. "x < 3" forgets the negative side.

Chemistry13. The maximum number of electrons in the n = 3 energy level is

2n2 = 2 × 9 = 18 (3s, 3p and 3d). 8 is a common confusion with the period length; 9 is the number of orbitals.

Maths14. $5000 is invested at 4% compounded annually. Its value after 2 years is

5000 × 1.042 = 5000 × 1.0816 = $5408. $5400 is simple interest (4% of the original twice).

Chemistry15. The condensed electron configuration of Fe2+ (Z = 26) is

Fe is [Ar] 4s2 3d6, and transition metal ions lose the 4s electrons first, leaving [Ar] 3d6.

Maths16. A car worth $30 000 loses 20% of its value each year. Its value after 2 years is

30 000 × 0.82 = 30 000 × 0.64 = $19 200. $18 000 takes 20% of the original price twice.

Chemistry17. How many unpaired electrons does a nitrogen atom have?

2p3: by Hund's rule the three p electrons occupy separate orbitals with parallel spins, so all three are unpaired.

Maths18. Which symbol shows that a statement is true for every value of x?

≡ means "is identical to" (an identity). = holds only for particular values.

Chemistry19. (HL) Successive ionisation energies (kJ mol−1) of an element: 800, 2427, 3660, 25026, 32827. Its group is

The big jump comes after the 3rd electron, so there are three outer electrons: group 13 (this is boron).

Maths20. Which completes a valid proof? "For any integer n, n + (n + 1) = 2n + 1, so the sum of two consecutive integers is …"

2n is always even, so 2n + 1 is always odd. An example (3 + 4 = 7) is not a proof.

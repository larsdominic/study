Before you start

  • Paper and pen, plus a pencil and ruler. Write on paper, not on screen.
  • No calculator. Put it in another room. Maths AA Paper 1 is the no-calculator paper, and every question below can be done by hand.
  • Formula booklet: yes. You get it in every IB maths exam. Open the formula booklet (Drive file "REF - Maths AA SL Formula Booklet (official).pdf") at the sequences page (un, Sn and S∞ for arithmetic and geometric sequences are all there).
  • The question paper: the sequences and series practice pack (Drive file "1.2-1.4 + 1.8 Sequences and Series (school 1.2-1.5) - Practice Questions (IB Questionbank).pdf"; on screen or printed). Do not open the mark scheme (the file with the same name ending "- Mark Scheme (IB Questionbank).pdf") until the timer has ended.
  • How long: 40 minutes on the timer, then about 15 minutes to mark and 10 minutes for the Error log. About 65 minutes in all.
  • Phone on silent. You can keep this page open on the phone: the timer works offline and stays correct if the screen locks (it counts from the time you pressed Start).

Mark it

Markingabout 15 min
  1. When the timer ends, draw a line under your work. If you want to finish a question, carry on in a different colour: anything after the line scores 0 in an exam, but it shows you what you know.
  2. Open the mark scheme at questions 4, 11, 16, 20 and 31 (c) to (g). Go line by line.
  3. How to read it: M1 is a method mark, A1 a mark for a correct answer or line, R1 a mark for a reason (31 (g) needs the sentence comparing the total with 624 000 m3). Marks in brackets, like (M1) or (A1), can be given even if that line is missing, when a later correct answer shows you must have done it. AG means "answer given": no extra mark, you only have to reach it. ft (follow through) means you keep later marks if you used an earlier wrong answer correctly.
  4. In question 20 (b) the table entries are un divided by k (the PDF shows them as fractions).
  5. Add up your marks per question and enter them below.
Quick check: final answers (worked out again by hand and with Python)

Error log

Error logabout 10 min
  1. Open the study sheet, Error log tab.
  2. Write one row for every mark you lost: the date, the subject, the question (for example "Seq pack 16 (a)"), what went wrong, and the rule that fixes it.
  3. Say what kind of mistake it was: did not know it (go back to the guide or handout), wrong method, careless slip (copying, signs, units) or ran out of time. Four careless slips in one set is a pattern worth a rule of its own.
  4. Timing: write down which question you were on when the timer ended. If you finished in extra time, how many minutes did it take? Speed problems and knowledge problems need different fixes.
  5. If you lost marks on 4, 11 or 16 again, write "repeat" in the row. A mistake made twice is a habit: turn its rule into a flashcard.

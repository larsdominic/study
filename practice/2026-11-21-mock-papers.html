Expected points Paper 1B (my own; the school mark scheme decides)
  • (a) [4] One mark per step: pull out the iron filings with a magnet; add water and stir until the salt dissolves; filter, so the sand stays on the filter paper (rinse and dry it); evaporate or crystallise the filtrate to get the salt.
  • (b) [1] iron 2.17 ÷ 5.62 × 100 = 38.6%; sand 1.98 ÷ 5.62 × 100 = 35.2%.
  • (c) [2] Error: the separated solids were still wet, or carried other material (for example sand stuck to the iron, or the iron oxidised), so 2.17 + 1.98 + 1.80 = 5.95 g is more than 5.62 g. Reduce it: dry each solid to constant mass (heat, weigh, repeat until the mass stops changing) before weighing.
  • (d) [1] The hypothesis judged by how much space the iron took up, but iron is much denser than sand and salt, so a small volume of iron has a large mass.
  • (e) [2] Fractional distillation; the two liquids have different boiling points (methanol about 65 °C, butan-1-ol about 118 °C).

5. English: one Paper 1 style analysis

English Paper 1 style analysis, one text
67:30
Ready.

HL Paper 1 is 2 hours 15 minutes for two texts, so one text gets 67 min 30 s (about 10 minutes of that for reading, annotating and planning).

The task67 min 30 s
  1. Choose one photograph from the New York Times collection (a different one from your visual thinking assignment), or a print advert you can see in full.
  2. Use a guiding question as in the exam, for example: "How does the photographer use composition and framing to shape the viewer's response?" or "How do the image and the words in this advert work together to persuade its audience?"

Error log

Error logabout 10 min
  1. Open the study sheet, Error log tab.
  2. Write one row for every mark you lost: the date, the subject, the question (for example "FA1 Paper 1A Q11"), what went wrong, and the rule that fixes it.
  3. Say what kind of mistake it was: did not know it (go back to the guide or handout), wrong method, careless slip (copying, signs, units) or ran out of time. Four careless slips in one set is a pattern worth a rule of its own.
  4. Timing: write down which question you were on when the timer ended. If you finished in extra time, how many minutes did it take? Speed problems and knowledge problems need different fixes.
  5. Add the papers to the Error log by subject. Then pick the three topics where you lost most marks today: they go first in next week's plan.

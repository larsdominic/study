Flashcards

The things to learn by heart from each study guide: formulas, definitions and exam rules. Five to ten minutes a day beats an hour once a week.

How to use: read the question, answer it out loud or on paper, then press Show answer. Press Again if you were wrong or slow, Got it if you knew it. A card you get right comes back after 1 day, then 3, 7 and 14 days; "Again" brings it back today. Each guide's cards appear on its plan day, at most 10 new cards a day. Keys: Space shows the answer, 1 = Again, 2 = Got it.
